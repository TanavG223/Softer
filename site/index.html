The games are optional distraction, not therapy, assessment, proof of calm, or a source of personalization data.

One to three noticing prompts move a lantern toward shore. Sound is unnecessary; Skip moves one waypoint and Stop leaves immediately.

No: score, speed, streak, randomized reward, or escalating difficulty.

Research boundary

“May offer a change of focus” is the whole claim.

Reviews find promising but heterogeneous, game-dependent results. Neither review tested PaceBack or guarantees an individual effect.

04

Repository-informed · deliberately original

Borrow clarity. Leave surveillance and pressure.

We compared public repositories and official products. They are design references, not endorsements or evidence that PaceBack works.

Medito

Broad content versus one immediate choice

Medito is a mature meditation library. PaceBack is a narrow native Mac tool with no content backend.

Breathly

One deep modality versus nine shallow ones

Breathly specializes in paced breathing. PaceBack keeps breathing optional and offers movement, connection, orientation, planning, screen-off time, and play.

The Baseline

Closest low-pressure philosophy

Both reject guilt and streaks. PaceBack differs through its moment-first flow, two finite games, encrypted multi-profile boundaries, and persistent support.

OpenCouch

No AI companion relationship

PaceBack has no chatbot, conversation memory, backend, or crisis classifier. Static support never waits for inference.

≠

Different by contract, not decoration. No account, journal ingestion, passive sensing, mood score, feed, currency, streak, chatbot, or support tracking.

05

Plausible mechanisms · untested product

How PaceBack may support wellbeing.

Mental wellbeing includes the capacity to cope, function, connect, and participate. PaceBack targets one small part: reducing choice burden and offering a reversible next action.

Orient

WHO stress guidance includes grounding and building awareness. PaceBack offers short outside-in noticing with an immediate exit.

Relax or move

NIMH lists movement and relaxation activities, including breathing and muscle relaxation, as general self-care options.

Connect or simplify

Social connection matters to wellbeing; choosing one small priority can make the next few minutes more manageable.

Change focus through play

Casual-game reviews report possible momentary effects, but results vary by game, group, and study. Harbor Tiles and Harbor Path remain unvalidated.

LIMIT

Research-informed is not research-validated. PaceBack can offer choices; it cannot promise that a person will feel calmer.
