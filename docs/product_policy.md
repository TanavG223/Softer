# Product policy

PaceBack supports a person through one ordinary stressful moment. It does not fix a person, define “normal,” diagnose a condition, provide therapy, or promise calm.

Required product rules:

- one obvious, age-available activity can start without setup or AI;
- every instruction may be skipped and every activity may be stopped;
- breathing is comfortable and optional, with no holds;
- movement stays within the person’s usual comfortable range;
- both games finish, have no score/timer/loss/streak/reward loop, and do not affect recommendations;
- personalization uses only a selected need and optional closed checkout;
- support stays visible and does not depend on storage or recommendation state;
- opening support is never treated as feedback;
- the app does not monitor danger or contact another person automatically;
- unreadable encrypted data fails closed.

For immediate danger or inability to stay safe, the interface directs the user to real-world emergency or crisis support. U.S. labels remain explicit; international users receive a directory link rather than a guessed local number.
