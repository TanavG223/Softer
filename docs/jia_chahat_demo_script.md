# Softer demo script — Jia and Chahat

Target runtime: 3 minutes 35 seconds to 3 minutes 58 seconds. Speak naturally,
and let every control visibly respond before moving on. Bracketed text is a
recording direction, not narration.

## 0:00–0:27 — Jia

[Begin on the Softer title, then reveal the Calm screen.]

“Stress can make even a small decision feel heavy. But many wellbeing apps ask
for more attention: an account, a questionnaire, a content library, or a streak
to protect. We built Softer around the opposite idea—reduce the pressure and
offer one useful next action.”

## 0:27–0:55 — Chahat

[Show **Continue without saving**, choose the adult experience, and enter.]

“Softer is a private native Mac app for ordinary stressful moments. A person can
start without an account, alias, or saved history. The name is also our design
rule: every path should feel easier to understand, easier to leave, and fully
under the person’s control.”

## 0:55–1:25 — Jia

[Open the need picker and choose **Thoughts moving fast**. Pause on the reason
and alternatives.]

“Instead of diagnosing a mood, Softer asks what might feel useful right now.
This choice is explicit; the app never reads sensors, typing, gameplay, or time
on screen. It offers one clear starting point, explains why, and keeps other
modalities visible.”

## 1:25–1:54 — Chahat

[Open **Gentle breathing**. Start it, select **Spacious**, enable shape-only
words, pause, resume, then stop.]

“Gentle breathing is an adjustable visual pacer, not a command. It can be
slowed, paused, or watched without breathing instructions. Reduce Motion
replaces the animation with a static cue, and Stop is always available if the
exercise does not feel right.”

## 1:54–2:35 — Jia

[Open **Harbor Tiles**. Place a piece, choose **Show a fit**, use **Undo**, then
place another piece. Briefly show **Harbor Path**.]

“For someone who wants active focus, Harbor Tiles is a short spatial puzzle.
Answers stay hidden, each cove supports multiple valid routes, and a hint appears
only when requested. Harbor Path offers gentler visual noticing. Both experiences
are finite and scoreless—no timer, streak, losing, leaderboard, or endless
reward loop. Stopping or skipping still counts as a valid ending.”

## 2:35–3:04 — Chahat

[Finish or stop an activity. Show **More settled**, **Same**, **Less settled**,
and **Skipped**.]

“This optional checkout can slightly reorder future suggestions on this Mac.
Only the response the person chooses is used. If an activity feels less helpful,
Softer temporarily cools it down instead of pushing repetition. Free text,
game performance, dwell time, and support actions are never used.”

## 3:04–3:30 — Jia

[Open **Need help now?** and show every support option without activating one.]

“Human support is separate from personalization and remains available if the
encrypted workspace is locked. Softer labels U.S. crisis routes, links to an
international directory, and lets the person open trusted-contact or
professional-care options. It cannot detect a crisis or contact anyone
automatically.”

## 3:30–3:58 — Chahat

[Show Privacy/About, then return to Calm for the closing frame.]

“We built Softer with SwiftUI, CryptoKit, and the macOS Keychain. There is no AI
model, chatbot, analytics, advertising, account service, or cloud backend.
Fifty-eight checks verify the activities, games, recommendations, support
isolation, and encrypted storage. They verify the software—not a mental-health
outcome. Softer does not promise calm; it makes the next minute smaller by
keeping every choice clear, private, and optional.”

## Recording checks

- Keep the exported video under Devpost’s four-minute limit.
- Record the packaged release, not a code mock-up.
- Keep the pointer visible and wait for each screen to respond.
- Do not trigger 988, 911, Messages, Contacts, or any external destination.
